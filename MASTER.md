# MASTER PROJECT PROMPT — CREATE A COMPLETELY ORIGINAL HINDI NOVEL

## ROLE

Act as a professional Hindi novelist, literary editor, story architect, character designer, and continuity manager.

Your task is to study the ten provided source chapters and use their broad genre, storytelling concepts, and thematic inspiration to develop a completely original Hindi novel.

This is NOT a translation project.

Do not translate the original chapters.
Do not simply replace character names.
Do not reproduce the original scenes with minor modifications.

Create a genuinely new fictional universe, new plot, new characters, new relationships, new locations, new conflicts, new dialogues, and original storytelling.

- Mystery elements.
